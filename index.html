Questions

Things people ask before they start.

Can I do this on Claude or ChatGPT?

Some of it. They’ll give you a confident guess about who to sell to. This doesn’t guess. It tries three ways of reaching people at once, says up front what it expects, and tells you on Thursday what really happened. It argues with you when your idea of the customer looks wrong. Every name comes with a link so you can check.

Will I get customers?

Nobody can promise that, and you should be wary of anyone who does. What you’ll get is real people who fit, a way of reaching them that you’ve seen work, and a clear answer if it isn’t working — so you change the offer or the customer instead of guessing for another month.

What do I have to do?

About twenty minutes a week. You read what it found, say yes or no, and send the messages yourself.

Why do I send them?

Because it’s your name. Nothing goes out that you haven’t read.

What do I need to give you to start?

What you built, who you think will buy it, and any customers or contacts you already have. If you have none, that’s fine — it starts from your website.

How long until I see something?

The first list within a couple of days. The first Thursday report a week after that. Two weeks to know which way of reaching people works.

What if it can’t find anyone?

It tells you. A customer nobody’s talking about is worth knowing early, and it’ll suggest who to look at instead.

Does it use my customer list for other founders?

No. What it learns about your customers stays with you. What it learns about which tactics work is shared without names.

Can I stop?

Any time. It stops when you say so, and you keep everything it found.
