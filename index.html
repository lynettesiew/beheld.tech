Is BeHeld right for your business?

Designed for founders and lean teams that

  • Have a defined offer
  • Can identify—or research—potential customers
  • Have not yet found a dependable acquisition process
  • Want more than a one-time strategy report
  • Are willing to take action and measure what happens
  • Can serve the opportunities the work may create

Not a fit if you

  • Want a guaranteed sale by a particular date
  • Want AI to act without your approval
  • Want high-volume, unreviewed outreach
  • Only want a generic lead list
  • Are not able to follow up with interested prospects

Potential fits include

  • B2B SaaS and AI startups
  • Consultancies, agencies, and fractional services
  • Recruiting and staffing firms
  • Commercial real estate and PropTech
  • Cybersecurity, IT, accounting, and financial services
  • Logistics, industrial suppliers, and B2B hospitality products
  • Wholesale brands and selected direct-to-consumer businesses

Frequently asked questions

Does BeHeld guarantee customers or sales?

No. Buyer decisions, market conditions, sales cycles, pricing, and closing ability are not fully within BeHeld’s control. BeHeld commits to clear recommendations, bounded work, transparent tracking, and honest decisions.

What will paid support cost?

Pricing is shared after the blueprint because ongoing support and execution needs vary. You will see the base scope and any execution estimate before committing.
