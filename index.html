Things people ask before they pay

Does this come from my email address?

Yes. Every message goes out from your own address, in your name. We warm the domain first and keep the volume low. Nothing goes out from a shared or rented sending domain.

What do you keep about my customers?

You keep it. Every contact, draft, reply, and objection lands in a spreadsheet that is yours, both during the work and after it ends.

What if the answer is “stop”?

Then we say so, and we say it early. Every run has a stop rule you agree to before we start. If we hit it, we stop, the rest of your budget is not charged, and you get the next path named in writing.

Do you test the offer, or just the channel?

Both, but not at the same time. A channel test tells you whether these people are reachable. An offer test tells you whether the reason to reply is good enough. We change one at a time, so the verdict means something.

What happens to the people who say yes?

A warm reply comes to you with a booking link attached, or we book the call for you. Nothing promising sits silent for more than a week. Closing the conversation is yours; keeping it alive is ours.

How much of my time does this take?

Five minutes to apply and 30 minutes on the call. After that, about 30 minutes a week to approve drafts and read what came back, and a little more when we are testing several paths at once.

Who is doing the work?

One person, working with you directly. There is no account manager and no handoff. Research, monitoring and drafting are agent-assisted; the strategy, the judgement calls, and every message that reaches a real person are not.

Do you guarantee customers?

No. Buyer decisions, timing, pricing and your own closing are not ours to control. What we commit to is a written path, work you approved, honest tracking, and a verdict you can act on. Neither pilot so far produced a sale; both founders changed direction because of what we found.
